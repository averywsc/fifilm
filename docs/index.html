What is included

  • Colour and LUTs: Primary correction, Lift/Gamma/Gain wheels, split toning, up to 6 stacked 3D .cube LUTs with tetrahedral interpolation, and automatic indexing of your colorstudies library.
  • Effects and scopes: Real-time WebGL2 film halation, Pro-Mist highlight bloom, chromatic aberration, vignette, grain, letterbox mattes, and live RGB parade, waveform, and histogram scopes.
  • Stabilization: Two-pass FFmpeg libvidstab with Smooth Motion and No Motion tripod lock modes, auto-crop zoom, rolling shutter handling, and Before/After split wipe preview.
  • Timeline and audio: Frame-accurate cutting, magnetic snapping, View All Layers, 48 kHz Web Audio mixing with fade envelopes and LUFS metering, beat and transient sync, and hardware NVENC / QSV / AMF / ProRes export.
